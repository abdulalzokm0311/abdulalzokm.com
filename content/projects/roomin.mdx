## The problem

Finding a roommate is a high-stakes decision made on very little information.
You get a few photos, a short bio and a conversation, then you sign a lease with
someone you met twice. The gap between how much is riding on the choice and how
much you actually know is where the anxiety lives.

Existing options mostly optimise for volume. More listings, more profiles, more
messages. That makes the shortlist longer without making the decision easier.

## What I set out to fix

Not matching. Deciding. The goal was to give someone enough grounded signal to
say yes or no with confidence, rather than to surface more candidates.

That pointed at three things: compatibility that is explained rather than
asserted, reviews from people who actually lived with someone, and identity
verification that raises the floor on who is in the pool at all.

## Reviews and verification

Reviews come from previous roommates and are tied to a verified stay, so they
cannot be farmed. Verification is a visible badge rather than a hidden filter,
because the point is to let people weigh it themselves.
