## The problem

Peer-to-peer lending asks an ordinary person to make a call a bank makes with a
department behind it. On Passafund, that call was being made on a credit score
and not much else.

A credit score is a good summary of a repayment history. It is a poor summary of
a person, especially a person who is thin-file, new to the country, or early in
their working life. Lenders were either passing on borrowers they would have
funded with more context, or lending on a number they did not fully trust.

## Three goals

I framed the feature around three things it had to do, and refused anything that
did not serve one of them.

1. Give lenders signal a credit score does not carry.
2. Keep the assessment short enough that borrowers finish it.
3. Present results in a way that informs a judgment without pretending to make
   it for them.
